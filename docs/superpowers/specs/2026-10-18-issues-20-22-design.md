## Part A: Issue #22 Bugs

### Why
Players lose games because of the UI, not because of strategy: turn time disappears before they see the board, a scroll gesture halts a company by accident, a stray tap gives up a Rights Issue, and the trade popup is awkward on phones.

### A1. First turn of a new day loses time or gets skipped

**Observed:** "timer is already ticking by the time we see home for a new day." With short timers (15–30s) the first player sometimes gets auto-passed before they ever see their turn. The owner believes the skip is a side effect of the time loss; confirm this during debugging.

**Likely cause (to confirm):** after `share_suspend` resolves, or when there is no suspend queue, `auto_advance` runs `day_end → dealing → player_turn` straight away, and `check_and_start_turn_timer` starts the clock immediately. Clients, meanwhile, are still showing the end-of-day overlays (ShareSuspendOverlay with the networth graph animation, any day-transition visuals), so the backend clock is running while the board is hidden.

**Required behaviour:** the first turn of a new day starts its timer only once clients are actually back on the main board.

**Constraints:**
- The turn timer and its deadline broadcast (`turn_timer_deadline`) stay authoritative on the server. Don't move timing to the client.
- A disconnected or stuck client must not freeze the game forever. Whatever gating is used needs a server-side fallback timeout, in the same spirit as the 15s `reveal_complete` auto-ack.
- `check_and_start_turn_timer` must still be called before `broadcast_game_state` at every advancement site (existing contract in CLAUDE.md).

**Builder's call:** the mechanism. Options include a new idempotent client ack such as `day_start_ready` (mirroring `reveal_complete`), with the server deferring the first turn timer until all *connected* players ack or a fallback elapses. A fixed server-side grace period is the less robust alternative. Use systematic debugging to confirm the cause before choosing.

**Done when:** across a multi-day game with a 15s timer, the first player of each day sees close to the full timer on the board and is never auto-passed while an overlay is still showing.

### A2. Share suspend misclicks

**Observed:** "the clickable menu is not separable from delta table, the list doesn't fit all screen and scroll leads to misclick."

**Current state:** `ShareSuspendOverlay.tsx` stacks the standings graph, six clickable company rows, and the read-only "Prices After Fluctuation" grid in one scrolling column. The clickable rows look almost the same as the read-only rows, and one tap sends `share_suspend` immediately.

**Required behaviour:**
- **Two-step halt.** Tapping a company row only *selects* it (clear selected state). A separate **Halt** button confirms and sends the action. Tapping another row changes the selection. **Pass** stays and sends immediately.
- **Visual separation.** Selectable rows must be unmistakably interactive and visually distinct from the read-only price table. The price table stays but is visually secondary *(builder's call)*.
- **No scroll misfires.** A scroll or drag gesture on mobile must not commit a halt. The two-step flow guarantees this. The Halt/Pass controls should be easy to reach without hunting through scroll; the layout is the builder's call (e.g. a sticky action footer).
- Works on mobile (<768px, 44px touch targets) and desktop.
- `ShareSuspendModal.tsx` (the older plain modal) gets the same two-step behaviour if it is still rendered anywhere. If it's dead code, the builder may delete it.

### A3. Rights Issue pass confirmation

**Required behaviour:** when the player presses Pass in the Rights Issue sub-phase, show an "Are you sure?" confirmation **only if all of these hold:**
- they are eligible, meaning they can buy at least 1 rights share (the engine allows `holdings // 2`, so holding 1 share isn't enough),
- rights shares are available to buy,
- they can afford at least one rights share.

Otherwise Pass sends immediately, as it does today. The disconnect auto-pass path is unaffected (no confirmation on the server side). Confirmation style is the builder's call, but it must be quick: one extra tap, not a long dialog.

### A4. Trade popup: Max / RI buttons and timer

**Current state:** in `TradeModal.tsx`, the Max and RI buy/sell buttons are appended to the `-5 / - / qty / + / +5` row, wrap awkwardly on mobile, and are smaller than the other controls. The popup covers the turn timer.

**Required behaviour:**
- Move **Max** and **RI buy / RI sell** to their own row under the quantity stepper: two equal-width buttons, full touch size (44px on mobile). Keep the existing colours (buy/sell tone for Max, amber for RI), the enabled/disabled logic, and the tooltip text.
- Show the live turn countdown in the popup header, driven by the existing `turn_timer_deadline` / `useTurnUrgency` so it matches the main timer's urgency colours.

## Part B: Issue #20 Room Management

### Why
Groups of friends hop between rooms. Today there's no way to leave cleanly, a stranger using your name can take over your seat, the host can start before people are ready, and you can only find a room by being sent its code.

### Decided behaviour

#### B1. Duplicate names (lobby only)
- **Before the game starts:** joining with a name that belongs to a **connected** player in that room is rejected with a clear error ("That name is taken in this room"). If that player is **disconnected**, the join reclaims the seat.
- **Mid-game:** unchanged. Joining with a seated player's name takes that seat back, as today, even if the server still counts the old socket as connected.
- Why lobby-only: the server can keep a dead socket marked "connected" for up to 45s (the pong threshold). A strict mid-game rule would lock the real owner out of their own seat while their timers run, which breaks the "never lose your seat without choosing to" anti-goal. The impostor problem actually happens in the lobby.
- The client must treat "name taken" as a final error: show it and stop the auto-reconnect loop rather than retrying.
- Owner chose this over per-browser tokens on purpose: rejoining from a different device must keep working. A stranger can still claim a seat mid-game; that's accepted.
- **Departed players:** a player who left mid-game (B4) can never be reclaimed by name, even under this mid-game rule.

#### B2. Ready (lobby only)
- Every non-host player has a Ready toggle, and the lobby shows each player's ready state.
- The host's **Start** button is disabled until every non-host player is ready. The server enforces this too; it is not just a UI rule.
- The host doesn't toggle Ready, because pressing Start counts as their Ready *(builder's call)*.
- New joiners start not-ready *(builder's call)*.

#### B3. Kick (lobby only)
- The host can remove any other player from the lobby. The kicked client is told and lands on the landing page.
- A kicked player can rejoin *(builder's call; this is a friends' game, not moderation)*.
- Not available mid-game.

#### B4. Leave / Exit room (lobby and mid-game)
**Not built:** "close room for everyone". The owner explicitly doesn't want it.

**In the lobby:** leave immediately. Remove the player from the room. If the host leaves, host passes to the next remaining player *(builder's call)*. The leaver lands on the landing page.

**Mid-game:** behind a **strong confirmation** (spell out the consequences: your stocks return to the market, your cash is lost, you can't come back). On confirm:
- **Stocks** go back to the market (company available-share pool goes up). Chairman/director positions are recomputed (`update_positions`).
- **Cash** is burned (removed from the game, not redistributed).
- **Cards** in their current hand are discarded and **don't count** in that day's card reveal.
- They are marked as left and **skipped everywhere**: turn order, Rights Issue / Share Suspend / Chairman-Director queues (including when they leave while it's their move in that queue), reveal-complete acks, the turn timer, disconnect timers, and dealing on later days.
- If leaving drops the game to **one remaining player**, the game ends immediately with that player as the winner.
- The leaver lands on the landing page and can join or create another room. They cannot rejoin this game. A later join with their name must not resurrect the seat.
- The other players see the departure in the game log, and the player board shows the departed player as left. Visual treatment is the builder's call.

**Never automatic:** inactive or disconnected players are **never** auto-removed. Leaving is only ever an explicit choice by that player.

**Implementation direction (agreed in design):** soft removal, not deletion. Keep the departed player in `GameState.players` with a `left` flag rather than removing them, so `num_players`, indices and IDs don't shift mid-game. Every place that iterates players or advances turns must respect the flag. Known touch points (not exhaustive):
- `helpers.advance_turn`: skip left players. A round ends when all *remaining* players have had their turn.
- `actions` rights-issue queue construction, `phases._build_chairman_director_queue`, and the share-suspend queue build: exclude left players. If the leaver is at the head of an active queue, pop them and continue.
- `phases.complete_card_reveal` / reveal-complete counting: compare against remaining players, not `num_players`.
- `phases.deal_cards`: no hand for left players.
- Card reveal: if they leave before `begin_card_reveal`, their cards are simply gone. If they leave *during* card reveal (after `reveal_data` was computed), the builder decides how to handle it, as long as the outcome is consistent for everyone and the game doesn't stall. Simplest acceptable approach: cards already revealed that day stand, and the leaver is removed from any remaining queue.
- Server: `get_active_player_id`, turn/disconnect timer logic, `broadcast_game_over` rankings (exclude left players, or list them as "Left" below everyone else; builder's call).
- `to_dict` / `to_player_dict`: expose `left` so the frontend can render it.
- Frontend: strong confirm dialog, exit-room entry point in the game UI (desktop and mobile), left-player rendering on `PlayerBoard` / `MobilePlayerBoard`, routing the leaver back to the landing page.

**Highest-risk area:** someone leaving in the middle of a sub-phase (rights issue, share suspend, chairman/director, card reveal ack). Cover these with integration tests.

#### B5. Public room discovery
- In the lobby, the host can mark the room **Public** (default: private).
- The landing page shows a list of public **lobbies** (not started) with the host or room name, player count (x/6) and ready count. Picking one joins it with the name the user entered.
- Games already in progress are not listed *(builder's call: they can't be joined)*.
- Freshness approach is the builder's call (a polled HTTP endpoint is fine). The list just needs to be reasonably current.

### Constraints (Part B)
- Room state stays in process memory with the existing TTL cleanup. No new infrastructure.
- Max 6 players per room (existing).
- Engine functions keep the `{"success", "message", "new_state"}` contract.
- New WebSocket message types go into `types/messages.ts` discriminated unions.

## Non-goals
- Close-room-for-everyone.
- Per-browser session tokens and device-bound seats.
- Joining or spectating games in progress.
- Auto-removing idle or disconnected players.
- Changes to #21 (Walkthrough). The tutorial may later need updating for these features, but that's out of scope here.

## Anti-goals (would count as failure even if it "works")
- A disconnected player gets removed or loses their seat without choosing to leave.
- A mid-game leave stalls the game: a queue waiting on someone who's gone, or a round that never ends.
- The first-turn fix moves timer authority to the client, or a stuck client can freeze the day start.
- Share suspend can still be committed by a scroll gesture.

## Testing
- Extend `tests/test_game.py` (or add tests next to it) for: duplicate-name rejection vs. reconnect, ready-gated start, kick, lobby leave with host transfer, mid-game leave in each phase and sub-phase, last-player-standing game over, and the first-turn timer gating.
- Frontend: `tsc -b` must pass. Check visually at mobile and desktop widths.
